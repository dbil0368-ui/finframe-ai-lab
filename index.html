✨ About FinFrame AI Lab

FinFrame AI Lab is a browser-based creative toolkit. You can use its image tools directly in your browser.

This prototype does not connect to a real AI generation service. Some features depend on browser and device support.

🔐 Privacy & Security

Your privacy matters. Here is what this browser-based prototype does and does not store.

What this version stores

FinFrame AI Lab currently saves a short list of recently opened tool names and timestamps in this browser's local storage. It does not save your prompts, uploaded images, generated files, passwords or payment details there.

Uploaded images are processed in your browser for the selected tool. This static version does not send them to a FinFrame server.

What this version does not provide

This prototype has no user accounts, cloud sync, server-side encryption, or payment processing. Your browser/device and any browser extensions remain outside our control. Do not enter sensitive personal, financial, medical or confidential information.

Control your recent activity

You can clear the recent-tool history saved by this site on this browser. This does not clear your browser history, downloads, or other website data.

No account or personal profile is required in this prototype.

Privacy-first promise

We aim to collect only what is needed. Before adding accounts, analytics, cloud storage or AI services, we will explain what data is involved and provide appropriate controls. Never place private API keys in this public website.

This is an informational privacy notice for the current prototype, not a guarantee of absolute security or a substitute for a legally reviewed privacy policy.
