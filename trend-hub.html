BUILDING IN PUBLIC
New here? Start in 3 steps
1. Choose your country in Trend Radar and explore a topic.
2. Select “Create with this trend” or enter your own topic in Creator Studio, then generate a brief.
3. Save the brief to Content Planner, choose a publishing date and time, and save the schedule.
