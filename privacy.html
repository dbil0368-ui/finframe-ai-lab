Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This is a launch-readiness draft for the VYBORA Preview. Before public launch, the operator should review it for the actual business entity, applicable law, contact details, retention periods, and final provider configuration.

Information we handle

  • Account information: the email address and authentication data you provide when creating or using a VYBORA account. Authentication is handled by Supabase.
  • Planner content: topics, creative briefs, content formats, publishing dates, and status values you save. When signed in, these are stored in the VYBORA Supabase project and associated with your account. When signed out, locally saved ideas may remain in the browser on that device.
  • AI requests: the topic, format, and audience information you submit in Creator Studio are sent from our server to Google Gemini to generate a brief. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information as a topic.
  • Operational data: hosting and security providers may process technical request data, such as IP address, browser information, and logs, to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use this information to provide sign-in, save and sync Planner content, generate creative briefs, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service. We do not sell personal information.

Service providers

VYBORA Preview relies on Supabase for authentication and database services, Vercel for hosting and security, and Google Gemini for AI brief generation. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can avoid cloud storage by not signing in, although locally saved content may remain in that browser. You can remove Planner ideas from within the app. Account deletion and requests concerning personal data should be handled through the service operator once a support contact and deletion workflow are published.

Security and retention

We use server-side handling for AI credentials and access controls for account-linked Planner data. No internet service is perfectly secure. Data is retained while needed to operate the Preview and should be deleted when no longer required or when a valid deletion request is processed. Exact retention periods and a formal deletion contact must be established before public launch.

Children and changes

VYBORA is not designed for children under 13. We may update this notice as the service changes; the date above will be revised when it does.

Contact

A public privacy contact address has not yet been configured. This notice should not be treated as final until the operator publishes a monitored contact method.
