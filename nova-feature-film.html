Important note

This tool creates a 120-scene story-driven screenplay plan with scene descriptions and sample dialogue. It does not generate two hours of finished cinematic footage or soundtrack. The short-video maker can export a brief animated storyboard sample; producing a full two-hour film requires creating and editing many separate shots and audio tracks. Keep this tab open while building or exporting the screenplay.
