Make captions clear and natural

A good caption adds context to the image or video. It can explain a detail, tell a short story, answer a question or invite a useful response. Avoid generic engagement bait and claims you cannot support.

Choose hashtags carefully

This tool suggests starting points from the words you enter. It does not measure hashtag popularity or guarantee reach. Remove tags that are too broad, unrelated or misleading, and use only tags that accurately describe the post.

Before you publish

  • Check spelling, names, product details and prices.
  • Make the first line meaningful even when the caption is truncated.
  • Use a call to action only when it makes sense for the post.
  • Check platform-specific formatting and accessibility.
